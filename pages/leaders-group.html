UKREiiF LEADERS GROUP

Connecting change-makers,
driving impact

UKREiiF Leaders Group is a dynamic forum where the UK's foremost regeneration, investment, and infrastructure leaders collaborate to exchange insights, tackle challenges, and champion action over words.

This exclusive network fosters deep connections, thought leadership, and strategic dialogue that shape the future of the built environment. Together, members contribute to a lasting legacy of innovation and impact within the sector.

Join Leaders Group

Membership to UKREiiF Leaders Group ranges in price depending on if you're an SME or larger organisation – membership lasts for a 12-month period.

Within the period there will be monthly in-person meetings primarily hosted at various venues including morning, lunch and dinner events with exclusive guests including Government Ministers.
